# PokéBonk Offline 1.4.0

Bundled game: **PokéBonk 0.10.36 / mwxeoqzj**.

- **Updated game:** critical captures, Meowth Shop's **Buy all**, and a touch-button transparency setting.
- **Local Trainer Card:** customize your nameplate, Showcase and partner, and display your **[OFF]** tag. Available original mood art is bundled; if an optional portrait was not supplied, the card uses the regular portrait without retrying a nonexistent file.
- **Seasonal Mailbox:** claim the original Season 1 and 2 titles and Season 3 frame collection, without online participation or ranks.
- **Direct co-op voice:** optional mute, push-to-talk and open mic with separate microphone volume. Audio never uses TURN. Enabling voice ducks background music to 10%, SFX to 50%, and cries to 40% of their chosen levels.
- **Android co-op layout:** two columns on phones where space permits, with the roomier layout retained on tablets.
- **Offline Rentals:** refreshed 1,104 public builds for 138 Pokémon. Browsing and copying plans do not require the website or grant items or currency.

**Before updating:** finish suspended runs in the version that started them and export a JSON backup. Use matching app builds on all co-op devices.

Install the Android APK over the existing app—**do not uninstall**. Fully quit the old Mac app before opening the new one. The app ID, save schema and signing identities are unchanged.

**Downloads:** macOS Apple Silicon/Intel DMG and ZIP; Windows setup/portable EXE and ZIP; Linux AppImage/DEB; Android APK. macOS builds are ad-hoc signed, not Apple-notarized; Windows builds are not Authenticode-signed.
